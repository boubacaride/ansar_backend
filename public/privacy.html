Privacy Policy

Ansar Voyage LLC · Last updated: August 21, 2026

Ansar Voyage LLC ("Ansar Voyage", "we", "us") provides Hajj and Umrah travel planning and booking services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, mobile phone number, email address, and details related to your travel booking (such as package selection and traveler information). We also collect basic technical data when you use our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and manage your bookings, respond to your inquiries, provide customer support, send account and service notifications and one-time verification codes, and comply with legal obligations.

SMS/Text Messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages, we use your mobile number to send transactional messages such as one-time verification codes, booking and appointment reminders, approval or confirmation requests, and service updates. Message frequency varies based on your account activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

No sharing of mobile opt-in data. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties or affiliates for any purpose. We do not sell your personal information.

How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, our messaging and hosting providers) and only as needed to provide our services, or where required by law. As stated above, mobile opt-in and consent information is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.
